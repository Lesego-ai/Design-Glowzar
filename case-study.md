# Case study - Glowzar Skin Care

**The brief.** A small skincare range with several product lines needed to look like one brand across
shelf labels, a printed pamphlet, product pages and merchandise - on a normal small-business print
budget, not a brand-system engagement.

**What was designed, solo.** The mark and its colour system; front labels for four product lines
(Brightening Cream, Carrot Oil, Dark Inner Thighs Oil, Stretch Marks Oil) in multiple pack sizes;
per-product pages carrying benefits copy against product photography; a two-sided pamphlet for print;
business cards; and merchandise artwork.

**The decisions that matter.**

- **The colour decision drove everything else.** The early collateral was black-and-white against
  marble. The later work moved to the brand magenta - `#9A1C89`, the code carried in the project's own
  colour note - and that change rippled through the labels, the circles the photography was re-cropped
  into, and the price hierarchy.
- **The pamphlet went through six versions.** Not decoration changes: pricing moved up the page, the
  call to action became a shaped panel, photography was re-framed. Two of those states are published
  here in `process/` because they show the change plainly.
- **One system, four products, several pack sizes.** Labels at 25 ml through 250 ml had to keep the
  same hierarchy while the amount of legal and benefit copy changed. The per-product pages are the
  same template carrying different copy, which is what made four print runs affordable.
- **Print reality.** The collateral pages are 2480 × 3508 px - A4 at 300 ppi - exported 8-bit sRGB.
  Honest about that: sRGB is right for high-street printing and screen, not for a separated offset
  job, and the work was specified for the print route actually used.
