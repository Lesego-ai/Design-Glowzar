Glowzar Skin Care

Brand identity, product labelling, print collateral and merchandise - designed end to end, and revised in the open until the range read as one brand.

The work

A small skincare range with several product lines and a tight print budget. The job ran from the logo and colour system through to shelf labels, pamphlets and branded merchandise, so the same mark had to hold up on a 125 ml jar label, a two-sided A4 pamphlet and a printed garment.

Iteration - the pamphlet through six versions

The collateral was revised repeatedly against the client's own reading of it: palette shifted from black to the brand magenta, photography was re-cropped into circles, pricing moved up the page, and the call to action became a shaped button rather than a line of text.
